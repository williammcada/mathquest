# Nightfall assets

Four newly generated pixel-style cinematic illustrations: evacuation bus cover,
radio desk, supply market and departing bus. Direction: rainy teal city, amber
lamps, school-appropriate suspense, no gore or logos. Created with the available
image-generation tool, without external artist or franchise reference artwork.

Music is original deterministic oscillator composition from
scripts/nightfall-audio.py: 64-second ambient and 32-second finale loops.
No recorded samples, vocals or third-party songs. Effects use Web Audio tones.
These are prototype scores, not Udio-generated songs.

Combat map and actors are canvas-drawn, separate from the cinematic illustrations.

Five 1024 × 1024 transparent sheets (4 × 4 grid, 256 px cells), produced for
[Nightfall Art Direction v1.0.0](change-specs/nightfall-art-direction-v1.0.0.md)
from the prompts in [the sprite brief](art/NIGHTFALL-SPRITE-BRIEF-v1.md):

- `public/assets/false-haven/machinery-v1.png`

**Source:** generated by the owner with ChatGPT image generation, using the project's own `city/props.png` and `false-haven/tiles-v3.webp` as style references. No external artist or franchise reference artwork was used.

**Post-processing by Claude:**
- Each object was extracted from the 1254 px originals and re-centered within a 232 px fit per cell.
- Machinery cell 9 (barrier arm down) was replaced with the owner-supplied single-cell regeneration.
- Roof cells 0–3 were made opaque and edge-blended so they tile seamlessly; cell 3 tiles left–right only.

**Accepted:** all 80 cells present in brief order, with no readable text, logos or gore.
