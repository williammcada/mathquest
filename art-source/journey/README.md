Monkey face/tail, gold circlet, red scarf, gold/red armor, brown trousers and gold-banded red staff. Detailed late-1990s arcade pixel art with crisp clusters. Landscape, 6 columns × 4 equal rows, transparent background, 24 entire poses facing right. Maintain identity, scale and common foot baseline; keep staff/tail/scarf inside each cell. Rows: idle×2/walk×4; staff combos×6; jump rise/apex/aerial/landing/hurt/down; special×2/recovery×2/victory×2. No words, labels, grid, decorative frames or scenery.

Wukong final edit: remove ONLY all red/brown gradient background from the atlas, replacing it with genuine transparent alpha; preserve all 24 sprites, colors, staff, scarves, tail, small glows, poses, cell positions and image dimensions. No new backdrop or drawn checkerboard. Clean opaque sprite edges except intended glow. Do not change positions, scale or pose. Both generated versions had alpha; the selected final edit is retained.

Raider generation: one original small horned mountain raider, stocky green-gray skin, swept-back ivory horns, patched indigo clothes, leather belt/wrist guards, short curved saber and expressive snarl. Rich arcade pixel shading with dark outlines, side view facing right. 1536×1024, 6×4 equal 256px cells, genuine transparent background, constant scale/baseline and clear padding. Rows: idle×2/walk×4; windup×2/strike×2/recovery×2; flinch×2/knockback×2/falling/defeated; landing/stand/taunt×2/guard×2. No poster, labels, grids or decorative background.
